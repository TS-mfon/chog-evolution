## Recording notes

- Show the chosen wallet only as a shortened address. Never record environment files, keys, browser storage, or signing/debug consoles.
- Use the user's already minted token #1; do not mint again for recording.
- Record contribution preview and submission only after the source fix is deployed. If the contribution remains pending, keep that status and cut to the separately identified completed mint claim.
- Existing artwork version 1 was published by `chog-trait-renderer-v2`. Call it “published artwork”; only call an image Gemini-generated after a successful Gemini publication with recorded provenance.
- If contribution content does not meet the quest, show the genuine decision; preview readiness does not guarantee acceptance.
- Hide long loading gaps with a visible “Later: finalized mint review” transition. Do not imply instant finality or acceptance of the newer contribution.
- Keep captions to two lines, use high contrast, and hold transaction/reference details long enough to read.
